## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the quiet luxury, craftsmanship, and tactile precision of high-end cigar lounges and vintage cedar humidors, translated into an ultra-accessible, modern digital inventory environment. It balances classic heritage with rapid operational utility.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Refined & Disciplined**: Conveys the prestige of aged tobacco leaf and artisanal cabinetmaking without visual clutter or gaudy ornamentation.
- **Immediate & Pragmatic**: Designed for store managers and cellar masters who need rapid visual inventory status, fast stock adjustments, and zero friction in daily counting.
- **Warm & Reassuring**: Avoids sterile industrial coldness by adopting creamy off-white foundations, rich aged-cigar wrappers, warm saddle leather, and subtle brass hardware accents.

### Design Movement
- **Tactile Warm Modernism**: Combines crisp modern typography and clean structural cards with physical-inspired, high-contrast touch points. It relies on generous white space, subtle warm shadows, micro-grooves, and physical affordances for high-frequency stock tallying.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from aged tobacco wrappers, natural cedar humidors, polished warm brass fasteners, and linen paper bands. High-contrast legibility is maintained across all states for inventory management under cellar or dim lounge lighting.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#2C1810` - Deep Maduro)**: Rich, near-black tobacco brown. Used for high-emphasis typography, primary actions, and solid active states. Ensures strict AAA contrast compliance against neutral backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#8D5B4C` - Aged Rosado)**: Warm terracotta and cured leather tone. Serves as interactive indicators, secondary button borders, selected tabs, and secondary metric labels.
- **Tertiary (`#B88A2E` - Antique Brass)**: Subtle, muted brass/warm gold tone. Used sparingly for badge accents, reserve designations, focus rings, and high-value status icons. Avoids metallic gradients; keeps color solid and flat.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FBF9F6` - Linen Cream)**: Ultra-clean, low-glare canvas base that provides warmth without yellow fatigue.
- **Container Base (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White)**: Crisp card surfaces that isolate product imagery and highlight subtle ambient elevation.

### Semantic Status Colors
- **Optimal Stock**: `#2E6B4F` (Forest Humidor Green) with `#EAF3EE` fill background.
- **Low Stock Warning**: `#C05621` (Amber Leaf) with `#FDF2E9` fill background.
- **Out of Stock / Critical**: `#A82824` (Sealing Wax Red) with `#FBEBEA` fill background.
- **Border Tone**: `#EADFD7` for structural grids; `#D8C7BC` for interactive borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes dual utility: `Noto Serif` carries editorial prestige and cigar pedigree across hero headings and category banners, while `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers geometric clarity, open apertures, and extreme legibility for stock numbers, SKU data, and interactive controls.

### Implementation Principles
- **Editorial Contrast**: Restrict `Noto Serif` strictly to major view titles, brand headers, and box line names (e.g., *Arturo Fuente Gran Reserva*).
- **Inventory Legibility**: Keep all numerical counters, tabular stock levels, pricing, vitola gauges, and action buttons in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with medium-to-bold weights to facilitate rapid scanning.
- **Tabular Figures**: Always enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` across tables, stepper counters, and stock badges to avoid jitter when quantities change dynamically.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around 4px baseline increments, prioritizing rapid touch ergonomics and multi-density view states.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`margin`) borders. Single-column card view with persistent bottom quick-action tallies. Table mode switches into a horizontal scrolling data sheet with frozen box-name and stock-stepper columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px (`margin-tablet`) borders. 2-column cigar box catalog grid.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with 40px (`margin-desktop`) borders. 3 or 4-column visual gallery card grid or high-density tabular view with real-time stock filters.

### Dual-View Modes
- **Visual Card View**: Generous grid spacing optimized for photo inspection of wooden boxes, warranty seals, and vitola details.
- **Fast Table View**: Condensed vertical row height (48px - 56px per row) with fixed right-aligned stock adjustment steppers to maximize scanned rows per viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical layering rather than dramatic artificial blurs, drawing inspiration from stacked wooden cigar trays and archival drawer slides.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FBF9F6`. Ground plane.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Table Rows)**: `#FFFFFF` resting on canvas, framed by a soft warm keyline `1px solid #EADFD7` and a warm low-spread shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover & Active Stock Cards)**: `0 8px 18px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04)`. Elevates targeted cards when adjusting stock.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers & Floating Bar)**: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.14)`. Used for batch update bars and humidor relocation drawers.

### Surface Outlines
All interactive surfaces rely on deliberate low-contrast outlines in warm neutral (`#EADFD7`). This eliminates edge bleeding and keeps text crisp in high-ambient-light retail humidors.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft (Level 1)** roundedness to echo the handcrafted wooden corners of cabinet humidors and box presses. It avoids toy-like, hyper-rounded forms in favor of refined architectural structure.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Base Elements (0.25rem / 4px)**: Checkboxes, badges, stepper increment caps, and table selection indicators.
- **Medium Elements (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard buttons, text input fields, dropdown toggles, and image wrappers.
- **Large Containers (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px)**: Product catalog cards, modal sheets, and quick-filter control bars.
- **Pills**: Exclusively reserved for status chips and round `+` / `-` stock modifier touch-targets.

## Components

### 1. Rapid Stock Stepper (`+` / `-`)
The core workhorse component for fast inventory audits:
- **Structure**: A cohesive horizontal unit composed of a decrement button, a central tabular counter display, and an increment button.
- **Hit Target**: Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile; 36x36px in condensed table views.
- **Buttons**:
  - Decrement (`-`): Cream background (`#F5EFEB`), Maduro icon (`#2C1810`), border `1px solid #D8C7BC`. Pressed state darkens slightly to `#E8DFD7`.
  - Increment (`+`): Primary Maduro background (`#2C1810`), pure white icon (`#FFFFFF`). Pressed state darkens to `#1A0E0A`.
- **Central Counter**: Displayed in `label-counter` with `tabular-nums`. Flashes subtle amber or green micro-tint on change.

### 2. Stock Level Badges
Uncluttered pill badges with distinct typographic contrast:
- **Optimal**: Background `#EAF3EE`, text and icon `#2E6B4F` (e.g., `● 14 en humidor`).
- **Low Stock**: Background `#FDF2E9`, text and icon `#C05621` (e.g., `▲ 2 cajas`).
- **Out of Stock**: Background `#FBEBEA`, text and icon `#A82824` (e.g., `✕ Agotado`).
- **Typography**: `label-md`, uppercase tracking `0.05em`.

### 3. Inventory Cards (Gallery View)
- **Container**: White background, `rounded-xl` (12px), 1px `#EADFD7` border, subtle shadow.
- **Imagery**: 4:3 aspect ratio photo container with a subtle inner border for cigar box lids. Displays factory origin band and ring gauge info as floating tags.
- **Body**: Box name in `headline-md`, brand/origin in `body-sm` (`#8D5B4C`), price per unit/box.
- **Footer**: Full-width Rapid Stock Stepper anchored at the bottom edge for thumb reach.

### 4. Fast Inventory Table
- **Header**: Sticky top, neutral cream `#F5EFEB`, border bottom `2px solid #EADFD7`, column labels in `label-md` uppercase.
- **Row**: Alternating subtle hover states (`#F8F5F1`), 52px height, baseline aligned.
- **Columns**: Box Thumbnail (36x36px) | Cigar & Vitola Name | SKU | Factory/Origin | Unit/Box Price | Stock Status Badge | Instant Stepper Control.

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bar
- **Field**: White background, border `1.5px solid #D8C7BC`, radius `rounded-lg` (8px), padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus**: Double-ring affordance — inner line `#2C1810`, outer glow ring in Antique Brass (`#B88A2E`, 20% opacity).
- **Search Bar**: Features a dedicated barcode/QR scanner trigger button on the trailing side for warehouse box intake.

### 6. View Switcher (Cards vs. Table)
- **Segmented Control**: Pill-shaped container in `#EFE8E1` with two inset icon-and-label tabs. Active selection switches with a crisp white elevated chip and dark Maduro typography.
